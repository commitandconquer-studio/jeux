## Games for everyone

Commit & Conquer was born from a simple idea: **everyone should be able to play good games**, whatever their age, country, computer or budget.

So our games are **free**, with no ads and no hidden purchases, and **open source**: their code is published under the GPL, so anyone can study, improve and share it.

They aren't blockbusters. But they are **finished, polished games**: a real low poly 3D art direction, custom-made sounds, clear menus, save files and tested rules.

## How they're made

- **Rust** and the **Bevy** engine: free, fast, cross-platform tools.
- A **shared library**: sounds, fonts and style are shared by every game.
- Sounds and music **synthesised by our own tools**: nothing is borrowed.

## Contribute

An idea, a bug, a translation? The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/commitandconquer-studio/jeux): open a discussion, everyone is welcome.
