If you choose to show the weather where you are (cloud button in the header), the city you type, or your position rounded to about ten kilometres, is sent to the free service [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) to get the current weather. This choice stays in your browser and can be withdrawn at any time from the same button.
