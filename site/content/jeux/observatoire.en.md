At night, on an astronomer's desk, a star chart is waiting for its stars. Place suns and moons: as many of each in every row and every column, never more than two alike side by side, and follow the “=” (same) and “×” (different) signs. A hundred grids in the Book of Constellations, from 4 × 4 to 8 × 8, each with a single solution you can reach by logic alone. A daily grid, the same for everyone, a Novice mode that points out mistakes, and hints that explain the reasoning.
