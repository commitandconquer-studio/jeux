> **Game in testing.** *Night Guard* is still under development: the missions, the flashlight balance and the lighting change from one version to the next, and the game cannot be played in the browser yet.

You are the **night guard**. The room is pitch dark; you only see what your flashlight lights up and the tiles around you. **Burglars** come in through the door, look for the objects you protect and try to leave with them. Shine your light on them and they flee. Let one leave with an object and the night is lost.

## Your equipment
Before each mission, the **flashlight workshop** lets you build your torch: a **lamp model** (brightness, colour), a **lens** (a short, wide beam or a long, narrow one) and a **battery**. You see the light you will get and the **maximum run time** with the torch on continuously. A bigger beam shows more of the room… and drains the battery faster.

## Rooms to explore
The game and its **level editor** share eight worlds (home, supermarket, arcade, factory, laboratory, dungeon, cave, graveyard), with floors and furniture you can mix from one world to another.

## What is still being tested
- **Lighting:** the beam shape and the light falloff are still being tuned.
- **Balance:** battery life and beam size are starting values.
- **Missions:** only three missions for now.

## The look
Scenery is built from **Kenney's CC0 models**; the interface follows a “night security office” mood.
