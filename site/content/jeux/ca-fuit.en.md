In the middle of the pasture, a pipe has burst and water is gushing out on six sides. The plumber toad has a deck of 70 mismatched zinc pipes: 2 × 1 cards, from simple plugs to five-way junctions. Place them against a water opening, matching every edge with its neighbours, opening to opening, wall to wall. Water flows by itself through everything that is connected; what spills into the void makes a puddle, and that's fine. Three cards in hand, one more whenever nothing can be placed, and the next pipes visible on the side. The fewer cards you place, the more you score, and plugging everything is worth far more than a network that still leaks.
