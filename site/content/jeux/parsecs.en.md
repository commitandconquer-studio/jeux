At the controls of a small ship, cross an asteroid belt twelve parsecs long. Rocks break into pieces, some are icy, metal or explosive, and a boss shows up every three parsecs. The pilot and Bolt, NOVA's little drone, comment on the crossing. You start with a single ship: every point counts, and your running total unlocks new ships and liveries in the hangar. And every day, a challenge, the same for everyone.
