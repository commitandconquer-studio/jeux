**À la carte!** (“on the menu / on the map”) is a geography game, **in French only**, made for learning while playing: no timer, no account, no ads, and it works **offline**, on a phone as well as on a computer.

## Guess the flag
First the flag as it is, with four choices. Then with **altered colours**. And finally in **expert mode**: you only see the **colour proportions**, with no shapes, and you must find the country by typing its name.

## Guess the country
A country **silhouette** to recognise: first as it is, then **rotated** by a random angle, then **flipped** like in a mirror.

## The right colours
You get a country and **four flags** whose colours are wrong, except one. Find the real one! On the easy level the colours are very different; on the expert level they are almost the same.

## Recolour
A country's flag is coloured all wrong: **give it back its real colours** using the palette. Easy with two colours, expert with fine shades.

## The mystery city
The game picks a French town. It shows you **three towns** and their **distance** to the one you are looking for. You type the name of a town: if it is wrong you get its distance (and an arrow towards the right one on the easy levels), one more clue to work out the answer. As many tries as needed, with a “Give me the answer” button. Games cover French **prefectures**, **sub-prefectures** or **world capitals**. At the end of each town, a **map** shows the distance circles and arrows pointing to the answer.

## Levels for everyone
Each mode has four levels: **Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert**. Ten questions per game (three towns for the mystery city), one to three stars, and a level unlocks with two stars on the previous one. Your stars stay on your device.

## The data
**Flags** come from the *flag-icons* project (MIT licence), **silhouettes** from *Natural Earth* (public domain), **country names and capitals** from *Wikidata* (CC0), **prefectures and sub-prefectures** from INSEE and *geo.api.gouv.fr* (open licence). The **Baloo 2** and **Nunito** fonts are under the OFL licence.
