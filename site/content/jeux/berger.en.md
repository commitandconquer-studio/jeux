You are a sheepdog. Sheep run from the dog and stay together: get behind them, on the side away from the pen, and move forward. Meadows that change, with trees, rocks, ponds and fences; from level five wolves prowl: rush over to scare them off, or bark. Twelve levels with three stars each (finish, finish fast, lose no sheep), a daily meadow, the same for everyone, and a random meadow.
