**The Sheepdog** puts you in the paws of a herding dog, in a miniature field seen from above. Sheep run from the dog but stay together: get behind them, on the side away from the pen, and move forward. Bark to scare them, or to chase the wolves off.

## The rule
Three stars per level: bring the whole flock in, lose no sheep, and beat the reference time. The campaign has **18 levels** (**54 stars** in all): 12 meadows, then the **desert** world (levels 13 to 18) with sand, cacti, oases and two, then three wolves. A **daily meadow**, the same for everyone with a streak of days, and a **random meadow** complete the game.

## The animals
**4 kinds of sheep**: white, black (more stubborn), lambs (small and fast) and rams (they charge the dog if provoked), from 5 to 26 per level. **3 kinds of wolves**: the loner, the cautious one (attacks from behind) and the bold one (faster). They grab a sheep and carry it off: make them run.

## Your dog
**6 coats** to unlock with the stars you earn: Border (to start), Caramel (4 stars), Snow (9), Slate (15), Ember (22) and Gold (30). The *My dog* screen previews them live in the scene.

## Everywhere
Keyboard, mouse or finger, plus a **mouse mode** where the dog follows the pointer without clicking. The game comes in **5 languages**, on computer and in the browser.
