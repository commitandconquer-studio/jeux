**Conga Line** (« À la queue leu leu » in French) is the new life of our Snake. No more snake: you lead a **caterpillar of little characters** following each other in a line. Guests wait on a pink ring, waving at you; catch one and it joins the line behind you. Don't bite your own tail!

## A board that gets trickier
A new special tile appears every **5 guests**: **obstacles** (rocks, cacti or barrels depending on the landscape), **holes**, **teleporters** in colour-coded pairs, **speed boosters** and **conveyor belts** you can't leave.

## Progress that stays
The total number of guests caught, **across all games**, unlocks **characters** (up to the zombie, the skeleton, the vampire and the ghost), **landscapes** and **tile types**. The menu shows the next unlock and the game-over screen what just arrived. Your best score is saved.

## The landscapes
Meadow, desert, graveyard and pirate island, each with its own scenery and music.

## The look
The scenery and the animated characters are **Kenney's CC0 models**. The lighting, the tone and the slight “miniature” blur are the ones from *Cross or Die* (the blur is not active in the browser).
