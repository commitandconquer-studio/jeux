Slide the tiles: two equal numbers merge and double. Can you reach 2048? The game takes place on a **scientist's desk** (graph paper, flask, ruler, atom) and, with each new power of two, an **“Eureka!” card** tells you a little science fact. One key to undo the last move, and a saved record.
