**Crossing** is a low-poly 3D crossing game: a toad, roads, rivers, railways, and a camera that moves on its own. Go as far as you can… and don't dawdle!

## A world you uncover
At first the road is quiet: a few cars, some grass. As your **points add up from game to game**, the game grows: trucks, taxis, motorbikes, buses, trains, golden flies, then an **eagle** sweeping a whole column. Everything unlocks: vehicles, worlds, characters, accessories.

## Five worlds
**Meadow**, **desert**, **snow** (ice floes and blocks), **autumn** and **Jura-Croak Park**. Each has its own scenery and obstacles; the first four have their **own music**, which speeds up with the difficulty and winds down like a stopping record when you die.

## A garage of 47 vehicles
From monster trucks to mopeds, with nods to your favourite films: a car that heads back to the future, a talking patrol car, a bat on wheels, a pair of Blues brothers… Each has its **own name** and shows as a silhouette until you earn it. Beware the **Speed-Bus 2552**: it races along and blows up as it leaves the screen!

## Jura-Croak Park
A theme park where the dinosaurs have been fed **way** too much: leaning palms, giant ferns, a worryingly cheerful lab, gates far too big… **Dinosaurs cross the road** (Mad Ostrich-Saur, Three-Horn Bruiser, Spiky Tank, Cranky Rex…), some harmless and cute, others not at all. Park vehicles roam too (Guided-Tour Jeep, Cautious Safari Bus…) and **a pterodactyl replaces the eagle**.

## Characters and accessories
Toad, rabbit, badger, raccoon, hedgehog, fox, panda, duck: **8 characters** to unlock, then **7 accessories** (cap, shades, top hat, pirate hat, crown, party hat, wizard hat) to dress them up.

## Spectacular deaths
Squashed, drowned, swept away, too slow, snatched by the eagle: every ending has its own effect, with **zoom-in, slow motion, screen shake** and bits of scenery flying.

## And more
Flies to catch for bonus points (golden ones are worth far more), flowers, logs and lily pads, rivers that can always be crossed, an announcement for every unlock and an end screen that counts up your points and your record.
