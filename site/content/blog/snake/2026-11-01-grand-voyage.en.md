Follow the Guide! gains a proper adventure, and its eleven settings have been entirely recomposed.

## A single adventure: "The Grand Journey"
Story mode now offers a single adventure, starting with a **tutorial in the public garden**: 7 levels, with Léa, Max, Zoé and Rémi. It then takes the line through the **ten other settings**, **6 levels each**. In all: **67 levels** and **38 spoken scenes** between them.

## Recomposed settings
No more scenery sprinkled at random: every setting is now composed by hand, small near the board, bigger in the distance.

- **Public garden**: a clearing in the middle of the forest, with a small camp and a fire.
- **Desert**: bare sand, big well-spaced cacti, a bivouac and three palm trees.
- **Ruins**: a paved esplanade, a colonnade and an arch.
- **Graveyard**: a fenced enclosure, rows of tombs and street lamps.
- **City**: four big streets crossing, wider roads and bigger vehicles.
- **Caves**: a room closed by rock walls, braziers and lanterns that flicker, glowing mushrooms and a small lake.

## A gentler endless mode
Endless mode has **five speeds**, from very slow to very fast, and the line speeds up half as fast with each guest. The rival guide and the trains now move one tile at a time, to the line's rhythm. And music is off until you have set it.
