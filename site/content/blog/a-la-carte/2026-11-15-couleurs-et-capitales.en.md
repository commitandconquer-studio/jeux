**À la carte!** now has **seven game modes** (the last one, "Hidden country", is described on the game page).

## Two colour modes
- **The right colours**: you get a country and four flags whose colours are wrong, except one. At the easy level they are very different, at the expert level almost the same.
- **Recolour**: a flag coloured at random, to which you give its true colours back with the palette.

## The mystery capital
The mystery-town idea on a world scale: **195 capitals**, three starting capitals with their distance, and one more clue with each wrong answer. Easy levels only offer famous capitals.

## A map after every town
At the end of each town or capital, a **map** shows the **distance circles** around the starting towns and the **arrows** towards the answer. A way to see how you could have found it.

## A more cheerful interface
The mascot **Globi** reacts to your answers in a speech bubble, with a **streak** of right answers, confetti, animated stars and sounds. Each mode has its own backdrop, and the **mystery town** mode shows a live map with one circle per town you try. Animations follow your device's "reduce motion" setting.
