Cross or Die has a new face.

## The menu
A translucent blue panel, the **Cross or Die** logo, your best score, your next unlock, your deliveries and achievements, then big coloured buttons: **Play** (green), **Collection** (blue), **Options** (purple), **Help** (orange).

![The menu](/img/jeux/traversee/menu.jpg)

## In game
A light bar at the top left: score, best score, and the progress bar towards your **next unlock**. When you carry a parcel, a card appears at the bottom right with the customer's colour and an arrow pointing their way.

## The end screen
The knocked-out courier on a wooden sign, the closing line in the red banner, your score counting up, your best score, and the bar towards the next unlock.

## A little "miniature" effect
A light blur on the sides, where the character never goes, makes it feel like playing on a little miniature. The character is also a bit bigger, to spot it in traffic.

## The worlds
Seven worlds rebuilt with Kenney's CC0 models: meadow, pirate bay, Halloween, Middle Ages, space, Wild West and futuristic city.
