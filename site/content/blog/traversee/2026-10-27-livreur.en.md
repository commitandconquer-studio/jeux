*"The worst delivery service in the multiverse."*

Cross or Die is no longer just a game about crossing roads. You are now a courier for **Cross Express**, a rather irresponsible company that accepts every delivery.

![A parcel on your back: the arrow shows where to deliver](/img/jeux/traversee/livraison.jpg)

- **Coloured crates** (red, blue, green, yellow) appear on the grass. Walk onto one to pick it up.
- **A customer of the same colour** waits at the edge of the field. A card at the bottom of the screen shows the colour to look for, the reward (+25 points) and an **arrow** that turns towards them.
- **A customer of another colour** waits too. Get it wrong and they let you know: *"Wrong colour. We really hire anyone."*
- Dying means losing the parcel.

Cross Express also comments on your career: *"Welcome to Cross Express. Life insurance is optional."* at the start, *"Careful, this train doesn't deliver."* at the first train, *"New problem: the sky hates you too."* when the eagle shows up.

## Achievements
Your total number of deliveries is kept from game to game, with six achievements to win: first delivery, ten parcels, three deliveries without a mistake, five deliveries in one run, five railways, five rivers. The menu shows your counter.
