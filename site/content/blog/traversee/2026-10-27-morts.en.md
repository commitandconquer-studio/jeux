In Cross or Die you lose a lot. It might as well be funny.

## A line for every cause
The end screen now shows a line **depending on how you lost**, picked at random from several:

- **Water**: *"The delivery fell in the water."*
- **A car**: *"Express delivery. A little too express."*
- **A train**: *"Your career has derailed."*
- **The eagle**: *"Your parcel took off. So did you."*
- **Too slow**: *"Delivery overdue. No complaints accepted."*
- **The void**: *"Your delivery went to another galaxy."*

![The end screen](/img/jeux/traversee/fin.jpg)

## Three effect styles
In **Options**, the **Death** line sets the effects:

- **Cute**: butterflies flapping their wings, hearts and fountains of light.
- **Normal**: the original effect, with zoom, slow motion and a giant word.
- **Silly gore**: ketchup in every direction, a big scream, and the car exploding in its corner.

![Cute style](/img/jeux/traversee/choupi.jpg)

![Silly gore style](/img/jeux/traversee/gore.jpg)

It's all cartoon: cubes, ketchup and nothing realistic. Normal stays the default.
