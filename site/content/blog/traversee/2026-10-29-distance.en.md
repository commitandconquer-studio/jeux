Cross or Die's progression has been reworked: clearer and steadier.

## A maximum that grows every 100 rows
Roads, rivers and railways come in streaks. The number of rows **of the same kind in a row** is now a **maximum** that rises every 100 rows:

- **up to row 99**: 1 at most;
- **100 to 199**: 2;
- **200 to 299**: 3;
- **from 300**: 4, never more.

Each streak draws a random length between 1 and that maximum, and a road followed by a river is still allowed. This replaces the old two-lane road at row 50. The more obstacles a streak chains, the **slower** and more **spread out** the cars are (speed ×1, 0.85, 0.72 then 0.62 for 1, 2, 3 or 4 lanes in a row), and speed and density slowly rise up to row 300. The other milestones are unchanged: first road at 13, lily pads at 20, logs at 30, first railway at 100.

## Something new every 250 points
Your cumulative points unlock something at a steady pace: the **truck** at 250, the **rabbit** at 500, the **taxi** at 750, the **motorbike** at 1,000, the **bus** at 1,250…, and the **eagle** at 2,250 (it can then show up from row 250 of a run). Between two worlds, the small unlocks keep coming every 250 points.

## A new world every 1,500 points
Every 1,500 cumulative points opens a world: **Pirate Bay** at 1,500, the **Wild West** at 3,000, the **Middle Ages** at 4,500, **Halloween** at 6,000, **space** at 7,500 and the **futuristic city** at 9,000.

The world changes every **400 cumulative rows**, across all runs, among the worlds you have won: with two worlds they alternate, with more it is drawn at random, never the same one twice in a row. A run picks up where the previous ones stopped, and **dying in a world means reappearing in it**: you leave it once its 400-row slice is covered. You always start in the meadow; there is no starting-world choice any more.

## Distance
A **Distance** counter joins the score and the record in the game banner. The score also goes up with food; distance only counts the rows you have advanced.

## The model you win is shown
When you win a vehicle, a character or some food, the **model appears on screen**, to the right of the "New model won!" pop-up.

## A one-time reset
With this new scale, **every player's progress starts over**: on first launch of this version, the total, the record, the statistics and the character are reset. This will not happen with every update.
