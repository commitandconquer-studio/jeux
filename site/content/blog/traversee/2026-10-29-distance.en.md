Cross or Die's progression has been reworked: clearer and steadier.

## A maximum that grows every 100 rows
Roads, rivers and railways come in streaks. The number of rows **of the same kind in a row** is now a **maximum** that rises every 100 rows:

- **up to row 99**: 1 at most;
- **100 to 199**: 2;
- **200 to 299**: 3;
- **from 300**: 4, never more.

Each streak draws a random length between 1 and that maximum, and a road followed by a river is still allowed. This replaces the old two-lane road at row 50. The other milestones are unchanged: first road at 13, lily pads at 20, logs at 30, railway at 100, first world at 200.

## Something new every 250 points
Your cumulative points unlock something at a steady pace: the **truck** at 250, the **rabbit** at 500, the **taxi** at 750, the **motorbike** at 1,000, the **Desert** at 1,250, the **bus** at 1,500, then the **eagle** at 2,000.

## Distance
A **Distance** counter joins the score and the record in the game banner. The score also goes up with food; distance only counts the rows you have advanced.

## The model you win is shown
When you win a vehicle, a character or some food, the **model appears on screen**, to the right of the "New model won!" pop-up.
