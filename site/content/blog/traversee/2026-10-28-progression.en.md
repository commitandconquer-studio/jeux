Cross or Die used to start at full speed. Now a run **builds up step by step**: each danger shows up in its own time, and food replaces the gifts.

## Dangers arrive one by one
In a run, new things appear depending on the **row** you have reached:

- **Up to row 12**: grass, trees, rocks. No danger, just time to get your bearings.
- **Row 13**: the **first road**, a single lane, always followed by grass.
- **Row 20**: the first **lily pads** on the river.
- **Row 30**: the first **logs**.
- **Row 50**: the **two-lane road**, then three lanes at row 100 and four at row 150.
- **Row 100**: the first **railway**.
- **Row 200**: the first change of world.

The number of road lanes in a row is capped: grass always breaks up the highways.

## Parcels come later
**Parcels** no longer show up in the very first rows: you have to learn to cross first. They appear **from row 25**, and an **arrow** now floats above the recipient so you can spot them from afar.

## Food instead of gifts
The gifts you picked up for points overlapped with the parcels. Enter **food**: fruit, vegetables, burgers, donuts… a different set **for each world**. Each bonus spins slowly and bounces on a spring, in a glow of coloured light, so it is obvious that it earns points.

## New things announced
When you unlock something, a pop-up under the counter says **"New model won!"** (vehicle), **"New world won!"** or **"New character won!"**, and the *Next unlock* bar starts again from zero. The **eagle** unlocks at 2,000 cumulative points, then can appear from row 250.
