title = "Website launch and games online"
date = 2026-10-15
+++

- The **commitandconquer.fr** website opens, in French and English.
- The games now play **right in the browser**, with nothing to install.
- Every game speaks **five languages**: French, English, German, Italian and Spanish.
- An **Options** button shared by all games: language, music, music volume and effects volume.
- **Ambiance**: the scenery follows the time of day, the season and the holidays, and, if you wish, the weather in your town. Each setting can be turned off in the Options.
