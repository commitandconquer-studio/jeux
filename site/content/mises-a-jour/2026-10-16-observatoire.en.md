title = "Two new games: 12 Parsecs and The Observatory"
date = 2026-10-16
+++

- **12 Parsecs** (Nova universe): cross an asteroid belt twelve parsecs long, with a boss every three parsecs. Your total score unlocks new ships and their liveries, and the pilot and Boulon comment on the journey.
- **The Observatory** (Eurêka universe): suns and moons on an astronomer's star chart. A hundred grids in the Book of Constellations, a daily grid and hints that explain the reasoning.
- Both play in the browser or install on Linux.
