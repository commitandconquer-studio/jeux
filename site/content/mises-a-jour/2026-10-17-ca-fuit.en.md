- **It's Leaking!** (Croâ & Cie universe): a pipe has burst in the middle of the pasture. Connect zinc pipes, 2 × 1 cards in 34 shapes, until nothing leaks anymore. Water flows by itself, overflows make puddles, and the fewer cards you place, the more you score.
- Plays in the browser or installs on Linux.
